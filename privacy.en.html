Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy describes how the Sapiora app ("the app") collects, uses and protects user data. The app is intended for students aged 16 and up.

Who runs the app

Sapiora is a student, non-commercial project. Contact for privacy questions: edusapiora@gmail.com.

What data we collect

  • Email and password — an account is required to use the app. Stored with our authentication provider Supabase (EU servers). Passwords are hashed; no one sees them in plain text.
  • Consent record — when you register, we store with your account that you confirmed you are 16+ and agreed to the Terms and this Policy (date and language), so we can prove it.
  • Content you create — flashcards, notes, study plan, quizzes, stats, settings and conversations with the AI. Stored on your device and also as a backup linked to your account in Supabase, so it is restored after reinstalling the app or on another device. Photos attached in the AI chat are not included in the backup and stay only on your device.
  • Content for AI processing — text or photo you send to the AI (tutor, cards, quiz, summary, reading text from a photo). It is sent for processing to Anthropic (USA), the company that runs the Claude model. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and automatically deletes it from its servers within 30 days. It may keep it longer (up to 2 years) only if the content violates its usage policy or if the law requires it. Transfers of data to the USA are protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Shared room activity — room code, member list, chat messages, shared decks and notes. Stored in Supabase so all room members can see them.

Why we process data and on what legal basis

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — creating and managing your account, storing and syncing your content, AI features and shared rooms. Without this we cannot provide the app.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — app security, protection against misuse and inappropriate content, and proof of your consent to the terms.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — if the law requires us to keep or hand over data.

Email and password are required to create an account; without them the app cannot be used. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

What we do NOT collect

  • Advertising or analytics identifiers (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel).
  • Location data.
  • Contacts, photos, or files unless you choose to send them.
  • Health or financial data.

Where the data is stored

  • Locally in your browser or phone (localStorage / AsyncStorage) — all your content, so the app works offline.
  • Supabase — accounts, the backup of your content and shared rooms. Servers for this project are in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Anthropic — AI processing (USA). AI inputs and responses are deleted within 30 days, see above.

How long we keep the data

We keep your account data until you delete the account. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), everything tied to your user ID is immediately removed from Supabase, including the backup of your content. Data stored on your device is also erased when you delete your account or sign out.

Your rights (GDPR)

  • Access — all your data is visible inside the app.
  • Correction — you can edit your content at any time.
  • Erasure — Settings → Delete account.
  • Portability — on request we send you a data export.
  • Withdrawal of consent — by deleting your account.
  • Restriction and objection — you can ask us to restrict processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Complaint — you can lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz) or with the supervisory authority in the country where you live.

To exercise your rights, email edusapiora@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Age limit

The app is intended for users aged 16 and up. The AI is moderated so it will not generate sexual, violent or otherwise inappropriate content. If you are under 16, please do not use the app.

Cookies

The app uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Only strictly necessary local browser storage (localStorage) is used to remember your login, settings and content.

Sharing data with third parties

We share data only with processors necessary for operation:

  • Supabase — accounts, database.
  • Anthropic — processing text and photos with AI (the Claude model).
  • GitHub Pages — hosting the web version (USA). When the website loads, it processes technical data such as your IP address.

We never sell or share data for advertising.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top and will inform you of significant changes by email.

Contact

Questions and complaints: edusapiora@gmail.com
