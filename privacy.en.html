Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy describes how the Sapiora app ("the app") collects, uses and protects user data. The app is intended for students aged 12 and up.

Who runs the app

Sapiora is a student, non-commercial project. Contact for privacy questions: edusapiora@gmail.com.

What data we collect

  • Email and password — if you create an account. Stored with our authentication provider Supabase (EU servers). Passwords are hashed; no one sees them in plain text.
  • Content you write — flashcards, notes, study plan, chat messages with the AI. Stored locally in your browser or phone, and in Supabase when you are signed in.
  • Content for AI processing — text or photo you send to the AI (cards, quiz, summary, tutor). In the native app (iOS/Android), the AI (Llama 3B) and text-from-image reader (TrOCR) run on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. In the web version (PWA) we temporarily use Groq (USA) — in that case the input is sent and Groq does not retain it after processing.
  • Shared room activity — room code, member list, chat messages, shared decks and notes. Stored in Supabase so all room members can see them.

What we do NOT collect

  • Advertising or analytics identifiers (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel).
  • Location data.
  • Contacts, photos, or files unless you choose to send them.
  • Health or financial data.

Where the data is stored

  • Locally in your browser or phone (localStorage / AsyncStorage) — flashcards, notes, plan, streak.
  • Supabase — accounts and account-linked data. Servers for this project are in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Groq — only in the web version (PWA) for temporary AI processing (USA). In the native app the AI runs on your device and nothing is sent.

How long we keep the data

We keep your account data until you delete the account. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), everything tied to your user ID is immediately removed from Supabase.

Your rights (GDPR)

  • Access — all your data is visible inside the app.
  • Correction — you can edit your content at any time.
  • Erasure — Settings → Delete account.
  • Portability — on request we send you a data export.
  • Withdrawal of consent — by deleting your account.

To exercise your rights, email edusapiora@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Age limit

The app is intended for users aged 12 and up. The AI is moderated so it will not generate sexual, violent or otherwise inappropriate content. If you are under 12, please do not use the app.

Cookies

The app uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Only strictly necessary local browser storage (localStorage) is used to remember your login and settings.

Sharing data with third parties

We share data only with processors necessary for operation:

  • Supabase — accounts, database.
  • Groq — web version only (one-off AI processing). Not used in the native app — AI runs locally on your device.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting the web version.

We never sell or share data for advertising.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top and let you know the next time you open the app.

Contact

Questions and complaints: edusapiora@gmail.com
